Authoritative Paylane Policies & Grounding Sources

The agent cites these approved source documents for all policy and operational inquiries.

Paylane Refund Policy & Eligibility v2026.1
FINANCIAL POLICY · VERIFIED
84 Citations

Authoritative rules governing automatic vs. human-reviewed customer transaction refunds.

Section 1: Auto-Approval Window. Self-service refunds are limited to transactions settled strictly under 30 calendar days ago. Transactions 30 days old or older require human escalation. Section 2: Monetary Ceiling. Auto-approval applies only to amounts less than or equal to $75.00 USD. Any amount exceeding $75.00 must be reviewed by support leads. Section 3: Duplicate Protection. A transaction that has already received a refund cannot be refunded again under any circumstances. Section 4: Pending Authorizations. Pending authorization holds cannot be refunded as funds have not settled. Holds release within 3-5 business days.
Customer Verification & Information Barrier Protocol
SECURITY & PRIVACY · VERIFIED
112 Citations

Security standards required prior to discussing card balances, transactions, or personal data.

Rule 1: Two-Factor Identity Match. The customer must provide both the exact email address on file and the last 4 digits of their card. Rule 2: Zero Information Leakage. The AI support agent must never disclose, confirm, or hint at stored card numbers or emails. If credentials do not match, state generic failure without identifying which factor was incorrect.
High-Risk Escalation, Legal Action & Fraud Manual
COMPLIANCE & FRAUD · VERIFIED
45 Citations

Mandatory triggers requiring immediate human support transfer.

Protocol A: Fraud Over $500. Any dispute involving suspected unauthorized activity exceeding $500.00 must be transferred to the Fraud Investigation Queue. Protocol B: Legal Threats. Any mention of attorney representation, CFPB, FTC, or litigation triggers immediate escalation. Protocol C: Account Closure. Requests to terminate customer accounts require human lead verification.
